## Activity 3 - SmartCare Problem Analysis
### Task 1 - Identify stakeholders
| Stakeholders                    | What do they need?                                                                     |
|---------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Patient                         | A service they can use to book appointments in a system.                               |
| Practitioner                    | A system to let them know who they are to see and what time                            |
| Operators (Appointment managers | A system that will will allow them to book appointments and manage patient information |
| Management                      | As the project sponsor and product owner, have a system that meets business needs      |

### Task 2 - Identify Current Problems
1. Duplicate appointment bookings - Through there combined spreadsheets, paper records and manual processes
2. Limited visibility of practitioner availability - This information in the case study sounds like it not recorded in one spot
3. Lack of reliable appointment history - Because of the multiple collection points at the moment, the no single point for this information
4. difficulty locating patient records - As they are sighting paper records, this information is unlikely to be digitalised

### Task 3 - Ask Client questions
1. Who is the Subject Matter Expert(SME) in the current process?
2. Is there a current process procedure or training guide
3. Thinking of Databasing principles, Do you have a unique Client NO, or primary key?
4. Who will be the project sponsor and the business point of contact to confirm requirements
5. Given duplication is a problem - What is in place to not duplicate appointments. Can appointnments overlap?
6. What is the security trade off the business needs - have they considered the ISO standard on data record keeping and the system requirments
